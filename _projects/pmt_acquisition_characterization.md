## A Foundation for Detector R&D

Reliable PMT characterization provides the foundation for my work on large-signal response, waveform correction, and array reconstruction. At PandaX, I led development and maintenance of a universal signal acquisition system integrating high-voltage supply, signal readout and separation, digitization, and storage.

## Platform and Measurements

- **192 acquisition channels**, with **500 MS/s** waveform digitization and **over 1,000 MB/s** readout bandwidth.
- More than **500 days** of stable operation supporting batch testing, calibration, and abnormal-waveform analysis.
- More than **160 R11410** and **500 R12699** PMTs characterized, including ambient- and low-temperature measurements.
- Waveform-processing workflows and a performance database for dark-count, gain, and afterpulse evaluation.
